Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about shelf life, cooking methods, portion planning, and dietary varieties.

Our fresh egg-based long pasta (Tagliatelle, Fettuccine, Spaghetti) keeps refrigerated at 2°C to 4°C for up to 4 days. Stuffed pasta such as Ravioli and Tortelloni should ideally be cooked within 3 days to preserve the moisture balance between filling and pasta sheet.

Yes, all our pasta freezes beautifully for up to 3 months! CRITICAL RULE: NEVER thaw fresh pasta before cooking. Drop frozen pasta directly into rapidly boiling salted water and add approximately 60 to 90 seconds to standard cooking time. Thawing will cause pasta to stick and turn gummy.

Yes! Our Orecchiette and Campanelle shapes are naturally 100% vegan, made solely with Australian durum wheat semolina and mountain spring water. We also produce small-batch gluten-free potato gnocchi and chickpea fettuccine on dedicated Thursday clean runs to prevent cross-contamination.

Because fresh pasta contains high egg content and moisture, it is richer and more filling than dry commercial pasta. For a main dinner course, we recommend 130g to 150g per person. For a first course (primo piatto) or rich stuffed ravioli, 100g to 120g is the ideal culinary standard.

Walk-ins are warmly welcomed! Our display chillers are stocked fresh every morning with our core shapes and sauces. However, our most popular stuffed ravioli and specialty colored noodles often sell out by early afternoon, so pre-reserving via our Products page is recommended.

We believe local provenance creates unmatched flavor. Our durum wheat semolina is milled organically in regional Victoria; our eggs are delivered weekly from free-range pasture farms in the Yarra Valley; our ricotta is hand-ladled fresh in Gippsland; and our black truffles are harvested in northern Tasmania.
