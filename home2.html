Our Process

The Traditional Preparation Process

Five deliberate steps. No automation. No compromise.

1

Select & Measure Ingredients

Heritage flour, farm-fresh eggs and pure water — precisely weighed each morning.

2

Mix & Knead the Dough

Hand-kneaded for at least 10 minutes until silky smooth and elastic.

3

Rest & Relax the Dough

Wrapped in linen and rested for 30 minutes — patience builds better gluten.

4

Roll & Shape by Hand

Thin-rolled and hand-cut into each specific shape — never pre-shaped by machine.

5

Pack Fresh & Deliver

Nestled into biodegradable trays and dispatched within hours of making.
