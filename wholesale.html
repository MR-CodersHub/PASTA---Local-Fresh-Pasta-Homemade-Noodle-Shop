From Order to Pass: 4 Simple Steps

We understand how hectic kitchen operations are. Our order system is friction-free with late order cutoffs designed for post-service tallies.

1

Tasting & Kitchen Trial

Our sales chef visits your venue with a complimentary 4kg sample cooler. Test our pasta directly on your line during your staff meal.

2

Trade Account Setup

Get approved within 24 hours. Choose a standing weekly delivery schedule or order flexibly via our simple mobile portal or WhatsApp hotline.

3

Night Knead & Pack

Cut-off is 4:00 PM for next-morning delivery. Our kitchen kneads, extrudes, and chills your pasta overnight between 11 PM and 4 AM.

4

7:00 AM Coolroom Drop

Our refrigerated vans deliver straight into your coolroom before your morning prep team arrives. Signed and verified with digital invoice.
