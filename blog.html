Authentic Kitchen Tests

Cook Like a Master Pastaiolo at Home

Three foolproof recipes formulated for home gas stoves and electric induction tops. Calibrated with our fresh shop pasta.

Classic Sunday Feast

Tagliatelle al Ragù Bolognese

Prep: 25m Cook: 3h (ragù) Serves 4
Key Ingredients:
  • 500g PASTA Egg Tagliatelle Nests
  • 400g minced beef chuck & 200g pork belly
  • 1 finely diced carrot, celery stalk & onion
  • 150ml whole milk + 150ml dry Sangiovese
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste & 300ml light beef stock

Chef's Tip: Add milk before wine to tenderize meat fibers and prevent tomato acidity from toughening the beef.

Vegetarian Elegance

Ricotta Ravioli in Burro e Salvia

Prep: 10m Cook: 6m total Serves 2–3
Key Ingredients:
  • 400g PASTA Spinach & Ricotta Ravioli
  • 90g unsalted cultured butter
  • 14 fresh grey garden sage leaves
  • 40g freshly shaved Parmigiano Reggiano
  • 30g roasted Piedmont hazelnuts (coarsely chopped)

Chef's Tip: Cook butter until milk solids turn hazelnut brown ('noisette') before tossing crisp sage leaves.

Roman Purist

Authentic Carbonara Romana

Prep: 10m Cook: 10m total Serves 2
Key Ingredients:
  • 350g PASTA Bronze Spaghetti Chitarra
  • 140g cured Italian Guanciale (cured pork jowl)
  • 4 fresh egg yolks + 1 whole pasture egg
  • 60g Pecorino Romano DOP (finely grated)
  • Coarsely crushed whole black peppercorns (toasted)

Chef's Tip: NEVER use cream or garlic. Mix egg & pecorino paste in a warm bowl OFF the heat to avoid scrambling.

Kitchen Diary

Morning Rituals: Inside The 4:00 AM Dough Room

While the city sleeps, our kitchen is already humming with the smell of toasted flour, warm tomato reductions, and fresh egg crates.

Checking The Humidity

Melbourne weather can swing 15°C in two hours. First task: reading ambient hygrometer levels to determine exact hydration milligram adjustments for the morning batch.

Cracking Yarra Valley Eggs

Over 800 pasture-raised eggs cracked by hand every sunrise. We check yolk viscosity and vibrant marigold color to ensure dough elasticity remains unmatched.

Brass & Bronze Extrusion

Our heavy Italian cast-brass dies are warmed slowly. Bronze creates micro-grooves along every ribbon cut that capture your sauce rather than letting it slip away.

The Nonna Quality Taste

Before any batch is sealed, a 100g test portion is dropped into salted water, timed to the second, and tasted plain with just a drop of olive oil. Perfection or restart.
